About Me

I am a London-based Data Scientist and Python Developer focused on transforming data into clear, practical insights. I work across the full data workflow—from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to visualisation and model development—building reliable, well-structured solutions grounded in statistical thinking.

I completed formal training through an online Data Science programme with Imperial College London, where I developed a strong foundation in statistics, analytical reasoning, and applied machine learning. I am particularly interested in uncovering meaningful patterns in real-world data and communicating findings in a clear, accessible way.

Alongside my analytical work, I design and build thoughtful digital products, including Aero Atlas — Terminal Time Screensaver and TradeHourPro — Live Global Market Hours. These projects reflect my interest in clarity, global systems, and purposeful design.

Influenced by architecture, design, photography, and videography, I approach both code and visualisation with the same mindset: structure, composition, and usability matter. Whether analysing data or building tools, I aim to combine technical precision with clean, intentional design.

What I Do

Development & Data Science

I build clear, reliable Python-based solutions, with experience in exploratory data analysis, modelling, and automation to support data-driven decision-making. I focus on structured code, practical insights, and solutions that are both technically sound and easy to understand.

Digital Products, Web Apps & Software

I design and develop digital tools, web applications, and small software utilities that prioritise clarity, usability, and thoughtful design. My work ranges from practical tools to creative interactive applications built with modern web technologies. Alongside these projects, I also create websites and experimental applications that explore themes such as time, global systems, and purposeful simplicity. Photography & Videography

Through photography and videography, I explore architecture and urban life across different countries and cultures. I'm fascinated by how light, form, and environment reveal the character of a place—small, fleeting moments that reflect something universal. Whether capturing still images or moving scenes, I aim to tell stories that are both intimate and broadly resonant.

Design

I am inspired by both historic and contemporary architecture, as well as minimalist, purposeful design—where form supports function and every detail has intent. Whether classical or modern, I'm drawn to structure, proportion, and the way thoughtful design shapes experience. I carry this approach into my work, aiming for clarity, simplicity, and considered execution.
