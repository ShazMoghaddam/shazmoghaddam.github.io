About

From raw data
to something useful

I'm a London-based Data Scientist and Python Developer, trained at Imperial College London (96% average). My day-to-day spans the whole data workflow: cleaning messy datasets, building and tuning models, and shipping the result as something people can actually use, not just a notebook that lives on my laptop.

Outside client and product work, I build my own things: Android apps, trading tools, generative art systems, live wallpapers, screensavers, word-cloud typography. Forty projects so far, spread across the Google Play Store, Gumroad, Envato, and Lemon Squeezy. Running these myself taught me things a job rarely does: how to price something, when to ship versus keep polishing, and what users actually respond to once a product is out in the world.

There's a quieter part of my life too. After major surgery and the birth of my eldest child, I started donating blood regularly, and in 2026 hit my 50th donation, recognised by NHS Blood and Transplant. It's a small, repeatable way of putting something back.

I also write, though not often and not because I'd call myself a writer. Some thoughts just need somewhere to land. A few of those essays are published on Medium, and you can find them in the Writing section below.
